## Council Debate

Each debate cycle follows this structure. All LLM members speak in parallel during
Phase 1, then the orchestrator processes the remaining phases.

### Phase 1: Response
All members respond to the goal in parallel. Each member uses their expertise and
judgment to respond appropriately — whether that means proposing an experiment,
challenging a claim, summarizing findings, or simply acknowledging the input.

### Phase 2: Challenge
Members challenge each other's proposals:
- **Skeptic** verifies evidence claims against the ledger
- **Analyst** identifies chain opportunities and blind spots
- **Operator** assesses feasibility and flags blockers

### Phase 3: Revision
Based on challenges, the strategist either revises, defends, or pivots.

### Phase 4: Execution
The operator executes approved proposals and records evidence.

### Phase 5: Reflection
All members reflect on results: what worked, what failed, what was learned.

## Principles

1. **One proposal per turn** when there is a concrete experiment to plan.
2. **Skeptic has veto** on claims lacking evidence.
3. **Evidence required** before any proposal proceeds to execution.
4. **Pivot on failure** — switch attack type, don't retry with minor variations.
